### D) Risk drivers and candidate indicators (proposed for future tracking)

Even without these specific diffs, below are practical indicators likely to be more predictive than PR size or off-hours merge:

1. **Critical-path logic change without test delta**
   - Measures: PRs touching critical-domain files (payments, auth, ledger) with no test files changed.
   - Relevance: Functional defects in critical paths are high-impact; absent test updates increases escape risk.
   - Compute: `critical_path_touched == true` AND `test_files_changed == 0`.

2. **Approval depth and discussion quality score**
   - Measures: Number of unique approvers + presence of substantive review comments/changes requested before merge.
   - Relevance: Detects “no-look approvals” better than simple approval count.
   - Compute: weighted score from `unique_approvers`, `review_comments_count`, `changes_requested_count`, `comment_response_ratio`.

3. **Guardrail completeness index**
   - Measures: Whether PR includes explicit safety controls (feature flag, canary/progressive rollout, rollback playbook, alerting updates).
   - Relevance: Reduces blast radius and MTTR when defects occur.
   - Compute: binary flags from PR text/diff labels + path heuristics; aggregate into 0–4 score.

4. **Risky change class + low-review interaction**
   - Measures: PRs classified as business-logic/config/infrastructure in critical systems with low reviewer interaction.
   - Relevance: High-risk change classes require high-friction review.
   - Compute: `change_type in {business-logic, config/infra}` AND `critical_service==true` AND (`review_comments==0` OR `unique_reviewers<2`).

5. **Post-merge defect feedback signal**
   - Measures: Incident-linked PRs where earlier PRs from same team/service had weak guardrails or low review quality.
   - Relevance: Captures systemic process drift and recurring risky behavior.
   - Compute: rolling 30/90-day rate of incident-linked PRs correlated with low guardrail/review scores.
